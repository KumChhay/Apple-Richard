QUICK TIPS

A few good places to start.

Having trouble charging?

Check the cable, adapter, and device port for a secure connection. If available, try a compatible cable and adapter, then restart the device.

Keep your software current

On iPhone or iPad, open Settings → General → Software Update. On Mac, open System Settings → General → Software Update.

Before service or setup

Back up important data, keep your Apple Account details handy, and check the exact model number when looking for accessories or instructions.
