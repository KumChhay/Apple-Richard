Richard's Day at the Beach

Richard brought his MacBook to the beach for a little work between swims. Beneath a shady palm, he opened it to finish one quick email. The ocean breeze was perfect, the view was incredible, and the screen was just bright enough to show a new message: “Are you still working?”

He closed the laptop, tucked it safely away, and finally went for a swim. By sunset, Richard had finished absolutely nothing on his to-do list—but he had found the best place to relax, and a very good reason to bring his Mac back tomorrow.
